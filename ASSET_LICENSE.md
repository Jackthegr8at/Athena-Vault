The upstream notices are from the Google Fonts Archivo and Playfair Display directories. Embedded metadata was inspected on 2026-10-07: Archivo version 2.001 identifies the Archivo Project Authors (2020), and Playfair Display version 1.203 identifies its Project Authors (2017) and reserved font name. Both link to the OFL; those copyright lines match the bundled notices. Redistribution and any further modifications must satisfy the OFL, including its reserved-name conditions. Middleware permits anonymous GET/HEAD requests to exactly these two font binaries and their two notices, while retaining proxy validation and protection of other paths. The built standalone responses were verified on 2026-10-07; repeat this routing check for the final deployed artifact.
